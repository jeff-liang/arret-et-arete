Failure Mode 1: Your interlocutor fails to make strong arguments so you conclude that you are correct. Unfortunately, reality does not always agree.

Failure Mode 2: You identify real problems but propose a solution that is ill-thought-out. The failure of the solution leads others to discount your arguments wholesale. See communism. This sort of "throwing the baby out with the bathwater" also applies to the Bible.
