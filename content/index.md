**Some Thoughts**
There is one big problem with web content which is trust. What you see may be made up or deliberately misleading to serve ulterior motives. Personally I've seen a lot of very tricky propaganda. It's dangerous out there. I hope this place will be a refuge from all that. I highly value truth and honesty, even if I am sometimes wrong!

That's the second piece that sometimes comes into play. If you're reading someone else doing some mental work like an analysis or a mathematical proof, there's also the question of their skill level. Even if they're trustworthy, they can easily be quite wrong if their skill is lacking. Here too, I believe I'm generally quite competent.
